I’m a software engineer living in Brooklyn, New York. I grew up in County
Donegal, Ireland, and studied computer science at Trinity College Dublin before
moving to New York.

Much of my career—including a long stretch at Google in New York—has been spent
building distributed systems and developer infrastructure. I have a soft spot
for functional programming, compilers, Unix tools, and the mathematical
foundations of computing; this site is generated with Haskell, Nix, Pandoc, and
Typst, as described in the [[./colophon.html][colophon]].

Outside of software, I spend a good deal of time with a camera or on a
bicycle—walking around Brooklyn, riding out to Fort Tilden or up into the
Hudson Valley, and traveling when I can.

I’ve kept a personal site in one form or another since 2005—including an early
[[https://jasonmc.wordpress.com/][college blog]] in Dublin and a longer-running
[[https://blog.jasonmc.net/][tumblelog]]—before settling on plain-text files
here.
